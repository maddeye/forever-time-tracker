# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Sorts your playtime into Fighting, Traveling, Gathering, Crafting, Eating & Drinking, NPCs & Trade, Dead, Idle, AFK and Loading.
- Window with a breakdown, a session timeline and daily history for the last 7 or 30 days.
- Views for the session, today, 7 days, 30 days and all time, for one character or all of them.
- Minimap button, addon compartment entry and `/ftt`.
